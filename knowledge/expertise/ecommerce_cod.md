# Cash-on-Delivery (COD) & E-Commerce Delivery Math

## The Hidden Delivery Rate Penalty
In Cash-On-Delivery markets (Middle East, North Africa, South Asia):
- Gross Orders != Delivered Orders.
- Typical delivery rates fluctuate between 60% and 82%.
- For every 100 orders shipped:
  - 15–30 orders return undelivered.
  - Return shipping fee is billed twice (outbound + inbound return).
  - Damaged goods rate averages 3–5% on returned parcels.

$$\text{True Profit} = (\text{Delivered Orders} \times \text{Average Order Value}) - (\text{Total Shipped} \times \text{Shipping Fee}) - (\text{Returned Orders} \times \text{Return Fee}) - \text{Total Ad Spend} - (\text{Delivered Orders} \times \text{COGS})$$
